# M6: Polish Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship the M6 polish layer: Wonder victory, a fourth AI level ("Mesteri") with seeded personalities, full save/load (5 slots + autosave) with a pause menu, a settings screen with volumes and camera-key remapping, and procedural audio (SFX and generative music). Every system works from the glass DOM UI, and every budget and art pin stays unchanged.

**Architecture:** Gameplay additions (Wonder clock, Mesteri, personalities) come first, so the save format is written once over the final state. Then the non-ECS state (AI internals, RNG, entity-id counter, fog, clocks) becomes snapshot-able. A Phaser-free `src/save/` turns a match into the CLAUDE.md §5.4 JSON and back; it is proven headless, by round trip and by deterministic resume. UI comes after that: a pause scene, slot lists, and settings in a separate persisted Zustand store. Audio comes last: one WebAudio graph on Phaser's context. Pure TS DSP renders the SFX into buffers, a live sequencer plays generated music, and an ECS watcher fires game sounds the way `CombatFxSystem` watches for effects. The watcher never writes to the ECS.

**Tech Stack:** Phaser 3.80 (WebGL, WebAudio sound manager), TypeScript strict, Vite, Vitest (node environment, no DOM library), Miniplex 2, Zustand vanilla, localforage, zod (new, sanctioned by CLAUDE.md §7.2).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-m6-polish-rulings.md` (binding, together with `CLAUDE.md` §4.8, §5.2–5.4, §6, §7, §9 M6, §10, §11.1). Background where the rulings point: `PROGRAM_TERV.md` §10 (AI), §12 (audio), §13.5 (save interface).

**Workspace:** `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-28-m6-polish/` (the controller creates it). Screenshots go to `shots/task-N/`. Measurement scripts are copied from `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-27-m5-eight-civs/shots/task-15/` (`tex-memory.js`, the heavy6 setup) and `shots/task-1/load-time.js`.

## Design Decisions (controller rulings made while planning — binding)

These are added to the rulings doc. The controller records them in its ledger.

1. **Task order:** Wonder and AI come before save, so the v1 schema covers their state from the start. `schemaVersion` stays `1` for all of M6, because no save has shipped yet.
2. **Seeded live AI.** `SystemRunner` currently builds strategies with `Math.random`. From Task 3 on, each AI gets `createStatefulRng(aiRngSeed(config.seed, playerId))`, with `aiRngSeed = (seed * 31 + playerId) >>> 0` (the sims' pattern). That makes the RNG saveable, and a seed replays its AIs.
3. **"Resume deterministically" means two things:**
   - (a) `serialize(restore(s))` deep-equals `s`.
   - (b) The same save, resumed twice, runs to identical states.

   A resumed match is **not** bit-identical to an uninterrupted one, because Miniplex query order depends on history.

   System-internal throttles are not saved; they restart as in a fresh match, which is harmless (at most one interval). These are the repath `WeakMap`s and `nowMs` in Gathering/Construction/Combat, the tower retarget timer, and the fog-scan phases.
4. **What the save holds beyond the ECS:**
   - map terrain **and** blocked mask (base64);
   - the setup config, plus a derived `meta` block for slot lists;
   - each player's population (written per §5.4, recomputed on load);
   - entities that are dying (kept, with their `ttlMs`);
   - `selectable.selected`, always written `false`.

   The dev `ThoughtLog` is not saved.
5. **Storage:**
   - Each slot stores a JSON **string** under `save:<slot>` in a `localforage` instance (`name: 'isorts', storeName: 'saves'`).
   - A slot list reads the full saves (≤ 6).
   - There is no slot delete (YAGNI).
6. **Remappable keys:** only the four camera pan actions, because the game has no other keyboard hotkeys yet. The keymap is the seam future hotkeys join.
   - Fixed keys: Esc and the arrow keys (a secondary pan).
   - Reserved codes cannot be bound.
   - Binding a key that another action holds **swaps** the two, and the card says so.
   - A binding stores `KeyboardEvent.code` plus the `key` label captured at bind time, so QWERTZ shows the right letter.
7. **Audio graph:**
   - One `AudioContext`, taken from Phaser's `WebAudioSoundManager`, which already handles the gesture unlock. Our own gain graph runs on it: master → music / sfx / notifications.
   - SFX are rendered by **pure TS DSP into `Float32Array`**, so they are testable in node. This is equivalent to the rulings' OfflineAudioContext.
   - Music is **scheduled live** by a sequencer, not pre-rendered. A 90 s stereo track would be about 32 MB of buffer.
   - Sounds requested before unlock are **dropped**, not queued.
   - No WebAudio means a silent no-op manager.
8. **Positional SFX:** gain 1 within 2 tiles of the camera-centre tile, falling linearly to 0 at 10 tiles. A sound on a tile the human cannot currently see is not played (no fog leak). Combat music is triggered by hp loss within **8 tiles** of the camera centre. PROGRAM_TERV's 5 tiles is too tight for a ~25-tile view.
9. **Wonder in conquest-only mode:** the Wonder can still be built, but `WonderSystem` is inactive: no countdown, no notice, and the AI does not build one.
   - The HUD shows every finished, standing Wonder whatever the fog (AoE convention).
   - A defeated owner's Wonder never wins and leaves the HUD.
   - Any known enemy Wonder overrides the AI's `enemyPlayerId` targeting.
10. **Mesteri** keeps Hard's 1.2 gather handicap (a rate, not free resources; PROGRAM_TERV §10.3/§10.4). It gets no extra vision and no resource grants.
11. **Personalities** (rusher / boomer / turtle) apply to Medium, Hard and Mesteri. Easy stays the §10.1 random builder.
12. **`src/i18n/hu.ts` may exceed 400 lines.** It is a data table; the file-size rule is about logic.
13. **Dev server for browser checks:** `npm run dev -- --port 5196 --strictPort`, then `http://localhost:5196/`. `vite.config.ts` keeps 5173.
14. **Pause:**
    - Pausing stops `GameScene` and `HUDScene` (`scene.pause`), so game time and autosave stop. Music ducks ×0.5.
    - "Kilépés a főmenübe" asks for an inline confirmation inside the card.
    - When a pause opens on top of a placement, the placement is cancelled first. A first Esc cancels placement; only an Esc with nothing to cancel opens the pause.

## Global Constraints

These bind every task. Reviewers check them.

1. **Stack:** Phaser 3.80 `type: Phaser.WEBGL`; TypeScript `strict`, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, `exactOptionalPropertyTypes`; `any` is forbidden. The only new dependency is `zod` (`^3.23.8`, Task 0).
2. **Language:**
   - Code, identifiers, comments and commits are in English.
   - **Every player-visible string lives in `src/i18n/hu.ts`.**
   - No browser dialogs (`alert`, `confirm`, `prompt`): every confirmation is inside the glass UI.
3. **Architecture:**
   - The ECS is the game truth; Phaser objects are render proxies; Zustand holds UI state only. The settings store is UI state too.
   - Audio, UI and save code read the ECS and never change a gameplay outcome.
   - No civ id appears in `src/ecs`, `src/ai`, `src/game`, `src/save`, `src/audio` or `src/config/balance.ts` (`civSurface.test.ts` guards this).
4. **Balance values** live in `src/config/balance.ts` and the `aiBalance.ts` family (`aiMedium.ts`, `aiHard.ts`, the new `aiMaster.ts` and `aiPersonality.ts`). Audio and music constants live in `src/config/audio.ts`; key and autosave constants in `src/config/constants.ts`.
5. **Files stay under ~400 lines** (except `hu.ts`, Decision 12). `GameScene.ts` (367 today) must not grow past 400: new wiring goes to helpers under `src/scenes/game/`.
6. **Tests:**
   - Pure logic is test-first in Vitest. There is no DOM in tests, so the UI gets pure view-model helpers that are tested, and the DOM is checked in the browser.
   - At the end of every task, all of these pass: `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run build`.
   - Sims keep explicit per-test timeouts and fixed seeds, and stay deterministic. Every existing sim passes unchanged in what it asserts.
7. **Art pins unchanged:** no art changes in M6, except new UI. Every `*Pinned.test.ts` and `hungarian*.test.ts` passes untouched.
8. **Budgets (CLAUDE.md §10, M5 figures):**
   - **Texture memory:** ≤ 256 MB for distinct6 and heavy6; ≤ 226 MB for twoCivM4. Textures only.
   - **Frame time:** 60 FPS (p95 ≤ 16.7 ms) on 96×96 with 4 players.
   - **Entities:** ≤ 500.
   - **Bundle:** < 2 MB gzip (M5: 0.64 MB).
   - **Load:** loading a save at ages 1–3 stays within the same budgets.
   - **Audio:** SFX buffers ≤ 8 MB in total; ≤ 24 simultaneous SFX voices; music scheduling ≤ 0.3 ms/frame on average.
9. **Browser rules:**
   - Audio starts only after the first user gesture.
   - Every `localStorage` access, including reading `window.localStorage` itself, is wrapped in try/catch.
   - Storage failures produce a Hungarian message, never an uncaught error.
10. **Visual direction:** warm dark glass DOM panels (amber `#f5a857`, Inter + Cinzel), with entrance animations matching `screens.css` / `setup.css`.
11. **Git:** conventional commits on `feat/M6-polish`. Tasks commit but **do not push**; the controller pushes.
12. **Browser checks:** run them on port 5196 (Decision 13); `window.__game` is available in dev. Each UI task saves screenshots at 1280×720 in `shots/task-N/`, and there are no console errors.

## Review Focus

1. **Loading a save taken mid-action:** mid-construction, mid-attack (projectile in flight), with a dying building, or with a defeated AI. The game continues:
   - the site finishes;
   - the shot lands;
   - the rubble clears and frees its tiles;
   - the defeated AI stays defeated and changes no victory result.

   Tests: Task 4 Step 5.
2. **A save the code cannot use:** an older or newer schema, truncated JSON, wrong shapes, bad base64 lengths, or IndexedDB unavailable (private mode). The slot row shows a Hungarian reason, other slots keep working, and nothing throws.

   Tests: Task 4 Step 7 and Task 5 Step 1.
3. **AudioContext locked, suspended or absent:** no exception. Sounds are silent until unlock, and no burst of stored-up sounds plays after the first click. 200 units fighting stays within the voice caps.

   Tests: Task 8 Steps 1 and 3.
4. **A remapped key that collides:** with another action, with a reserved key (Esc, arrows, Tab, modifiers), or with typing in the seed field. The result is a swap with a notice, a refusal, Esc always pausing, and no camera pan while an input has focus. A corrupt settings blob falls back to the defaults.

   Tests: Task 7 Steps 1 and 3.
5. **Wonder edge cases:**
   - A Wonder destroyed at 9:59 stops its clock.
   - A Wonder whose owner loses by conquest leaves the HUD and never wins.
   - With two Wonders, the first to reach 10:00 wins.
   - In conquest-only mode there is no countdown.

   Tests: Task 1 Steps 1 and 3.
