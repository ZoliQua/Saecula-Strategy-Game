# M19 — Regicide and King of the Hill Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two new victory modes, Regicide and King of the Hill, playable against every AI level; saves in schema 13.

**Architecture:** Both modes are new `VictoryMode` values read through predicates. The king is a base unit type without a trainer that draws with the civ's knight-slot sheet. The hill is an ownerless entity with a `hill` component, advanced by `HillSystem`; its zone is a no-build mask on `MapData`. The AI changes sit at the existing choke points (`ArmyControl` targeting, `attackReady` callers).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, Phaser 3, Miniplex ECS, Vitest, zod saves.

**Spec:** CLAUDE.md §9 "M19 — Királygyilkos és A domb ura" (Hungarian, binding).

## Global Constraints

- Hungarian UI text only in `src/i18n/hu.ts`; code, comments and commits in English.
- Numbers only in `src/config/balance.ts` (`KING`, `HILL`) and the AI config files.
- TS strict, no `any`; no new dependency; no new top-level folder.
- The old victory modes play bit-identically: no moved sim seed, pinned art or snapshot. No extra RNG draw and no changed iteration order outside the two modes.
- Map generation is unchanged.
- AI perception only through fog. The hill position and clocks are public, as for the human.
- No new unit sheet. Texture memory grows by at most 1 MB.
- Never touch port 5173 (browser checks on 5196). Never use bare `git stash`. Never `git add -A` (`node_modules` is an untracked symlink).

## Review Focus

- A king inside a transport is alive; the transport sinking defeats its owner in that tick (Task 2).
- A save and load mid-match keeps the hill clocks, the no-build zone and the defeats (Task 3).
- Switching template or loading remembered setup never starts `hill` on an unsupported map (Tasks 1, 4).
- A defeated player's or an ally's units never contest or win the hill wrongly (Task 3).
- AI waves do not oscillate at the hill or march off after clearing it, and a match cannot stall (Task 5).
