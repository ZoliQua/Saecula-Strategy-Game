# Code of Conduct

**English** · [Magyar](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.hu.md)

## Our pledge

Saecula is a small hobby project, and we want everyone who takes part — by
playing, reporting bugs, discussing ideas or writing code — to feel welcome.
We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

The game portrays historical nations. Discuss history with respect: no
nation, people or group is a target for mockery or hatred here.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and languages (English and
  Hungarian are both welcome).
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; criticise ideas and code,
  never people.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and
  learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages — and when someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately (see the contact details on the [@ZoliQua](https://github.com/ZoliQua)
GitHub profile), or use GitHub's built-in “Report content” option. Every report
is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is
respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, depending on the severity of the behaviour:

1. **Correct** — a private, written note explaining the problem.
2. **Warn** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporarily ban** — a time-limited ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanently ban** — for a pattern of violations or a serious incident.

Inappropriate comments, commits, code or issues may be edited or removed.
